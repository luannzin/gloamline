# References

The images live in `refs/` (gitignored: they belong to their studios and are for looking at, never
for shipping). Run `docs/refs.sh` to fetch them again. What each one teaches is below; DESIGN.md
says what Zimovye takes from it.

## Fidelity and light: Elden Ring

`refs/elden-ring/` (10 Steam screenshots, app 1245620).

- **One lit landmark, seen from everywhere.** The golden tree is the brightest, warmest thing in
  almost every frame and the player's compass. Everything else is desaturated toward the sky.
- **Air in layers.** Valleys fill with fog; each ridge further away is paler and bluer, so depth reads
  in three or four flat planes, like stage flats.
- **Silhouette first.** Castles, dead trees and creatures are composed as dark shapes against bright
  fog; fidelity is spent where the eye lands and held back elsewhere (Akiman on "controlled fidelity").
- **Warm practical light against cold ambient**: a torch in a purple cave, a grace's glow in a night
  field.

Sources: [GamesRadar on Akiman's comparison](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/legendary-capcom-artist-says-elden-rings-visuals-hold-up-against-higher-fidelity-games-like-crimson-desert-because-the-viewers-imagination-is-constantly-stimulated-to-the-fullest/),
[Automaton on the same](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/this-is-what-it-means-to-be-good-at-art-capcom-veteran-akiman-on-why-elden-rings-environments-stand-out-even-among-higher-fidelity-aaas/),

## Interface: GTA VI

`refs/gta6-hud/gta6-hud-1.jpg` is a frame of Rockstar's Extended Look footage (the minimap);
`gta6-hud-5.png` and `-6.png` are a community recreation for FiveM, useful for layout only. Nothing
here is copied: Zimovye takes the principles (contextual, corners, glanceable) and draws its own.

- **Contextual.** Meters, stars and prompts appear when they matter and fade when they don't; while
  driving, the screen shows little more than the minimap and the distance left.
- **Status meters top-left**, four thin bars, each with its own colour and a round icon, so they read
  at a glance and leave the minimap clear.
- **Minimap bottom-left**: a rounded rectangle, light and translucent, the route in one hot colour,
  the distance in the corner, north marked, the player a small triangle.
- **Weapon and ammo top-right**: a silhouette and two numbers. Notifications slide in low and leave.

Sources: [technerdiness, HUD explained](https://www.technerdiness.com/gta/gta-6-hud-icons-explained/),
[gta6data, Extended Look HUD](https://gta6data.com/gta-6-hud-ui-extended-look/),
[allthings.how](https://allthings.how/gta-6-hud-icons-and-meters-explained-extended-look/),
[bo3.gg](https://bo3.gg/games/articles/gta-6-hud-and-icons-explained-meters-police-profiling-icons-and-minimap-options),
[F5 Studio's FiveM recreation](https://github.com/F5-Studio-FiveM/GTA-VI-HUD-FIVEM).

What the bar is, in docs/CHARACTERS.md and DESIGN.md's Snow:

- **The blizzard is a place.** Flakes streak with the wind, close ones blur past the lens, sheets of
  snow cross the ground, the far trees go to grey silhouettes in a few tens of metres.
- **The body is in the snow, not on it.** Legs to the shin in drifts, a real trench behind, snow
  caked on the shoulders, the pack and the boots.
- **Layers that move.** Fur, straps, cloth and gear each have their own weight and swing.
- **The creature** (`refs/creature/`): a pale giant with antlers through its skull, raw red flesh
  where they broke the hide, against white fur in the white-out.
- **The survivor** (`refs/survivor/`): Metro Exodus's frozen ruins (snow-crusted kit, a figure from
  behind against a white void) and Misery's zone survivors (gas masks, a telnyashka, a padded
  tankist's helmet) as the wardrobe the district would have.

Still wanted: RDR2 snow stopping at a porch roof, a lantern in snowfall at night, a drift against a
wall, GoW Ragnarök's flakes (`refs/snow-world/`); a winter oak holding brown leaves (`refs/trees/`);
a real izba with carved window frames (`refs/buildings/`); a snow plume off a crest, a layered cliff
(`refs/mountains/`); Arthur's coat seams, a telogreika's quilting, an ushanka and mittens
(`refs/survivor-close/`); RDR2 snow with dry grass tufts (`refs/ground/`).

- **Snow is smooth and clean.** A pristine white surface with long soft swells; detail is a fine
  grain, never pebbles or spots. Its shadows are crisp and blue-grey, the brightest thing in frame is
  sunlit snow.
- **The trench reads by its shadow.** Knee-deep, as wide as the body, with soft rounded walls; the
  low sun fills it with shade, so the line of it is legible from far up the slope.
- **Legs vanish into it.** The boots are never seen in deep snow; the body is cut off at the shin.
- **Wind takes snow off the edges.** A plume blows off the crag's lee and off the ridge line.

## The engine side: abyssal-garden

[Noniv/abyssal-garden](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden): a procedural WebGPU scene in one
file. Zimovye takes its principles (docs/PLAN.md): everything generated, zero per-frame allocations,
cached shadows, dynamic resolution, a dithered grade, and its verification gate: fixed camera poses
reviewed by a critic that sees only the renders, iterated until nothing above minor is left.

## What the survivor is made from

The one place Zimovye starts from outside data (`tools/blender/setup.sh` fetches both into
`tools/blender/out`, which git ignores):

- **The body:** [MPFB](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/mpfb/) 2.0.18, MakeHuman's Blender
  add-on (GPL; the humans it makes are CC0), installed into a Blender profile of its own. Its `cmu_mb`
  rig names its bones as the CMU database does, so the capture maps onto it bone for bone.
- **The motion:** the [CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database](https://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/resources.php)
  ("free for use in research and commercial projects"), in Bruce Hahne's BVH conversion, from the
  [sfd158/cmu-mocap](https://github.com/sfd158/cmu-mocap) mirror. Acknowledgment, as CMU asks: "The
  data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with
  funding from NSF EIA-0196217."
