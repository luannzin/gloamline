## Fidelity and light: Elden Ring

`refs/elden-ring/` (10 Steam screenshots, app 1245620).

- **One lit landmark, seen from everywhere.** The golden tree is the brightest, warmest thing in
  almost every frame and the player's compass. Everything else is desaturated toward the sky.
- **Air in layers.** Valleys fill with fog; each ridge further away is paler and bluer, so depth reads
  in three or four flat planes, like stage flats.
- **Silhouette first.** Castles, dead trees and creatures are composed as dark shapes against bright
  fog; fidelity is spent where the eye lands and held back elsewhere (Akiman on "controlled fidelity").
- **Warm practical light against cold ambient**: a torch in a purple cave, a grace's glow in a night
  field.

Sources: [GamesRadar on Akiman's comparison](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/legendary-capcom-artist-says-elden-rings-visuals-hold-up-against-higher-fidelity-games-like-crimson-desert-because-the-viewers-imagination-is-constantly-stimulated-to-the-fullest/),
[Automaton on the same](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/this-is-what-it-means-to-be-good-at-art-capcom-veteran-akiman-on-why-elden-rings-environments-stand-out-even-among-higher-fidelity-aaas/),
[Game Rant on FromSoftware's art direction](https://gamerant.com/fromsoftware-good-consistent-art-direction-elden-ring-bloodborne/),
[TheGamer, best-looking areas](https://www.thegamer.com/elden-ring-best-looking-areas-locations/).

## Interface: GTA VI

`refs/gta6-hud/gta6-hud-1.jpg` is a frame of Rockstar's Extended Look footage (the minimap);
`gta6-hud-5.png` and `-6.png` are a community recreation for FiveM, useful for layout only. Nothing
here is copied: Zimovye takes the principles (contextual, corners, glanceable) and draws its own.

- **Contextual.** Meters, stars and prompts appear when they matter and fade when they don't; while
  driving, the screen shows little more than the minimap and the distance left.
- **Status meters top-left**, four thin bars, each with its own colour and a round icon, so they read
  at a glance and leave the minimap clear.
- **Minimap bottom-left**: a rounded rectangle, light and translucent, the route in one hot colour,
  the distance in the corner, north marked, the player a small triangle.
- **Weapon and ammo top-right**: a silhouette and two numbers. Notifications slide in low and leave.

Sources: [technerdiness, HUD explained](https://www.technerdiness.com/gta/gta-6-hud-icons-explained/),
[gta6data, Extended Look HUD](https://gta6data.com/gta-6-hud-ui-extended-look/),
[allthings.how](https://allthings.how/gta-6-hud-icons-and-meters-explained-extended-look/),
[bo3.gg](https://bo3.gg/games/articles/gta-6-hud-and-icons-explained-meters-police-profiling-icons-and-minimap-options),
[F5 Studio's FiveM recreation](https://github.com/F5-Studio-FiveM/GTA-VI-HUD-FIVEM).
